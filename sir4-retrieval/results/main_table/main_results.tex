\caption{\textbf{Retrieval performance (nDCG@5, \%).}
Same and Cross denote same- and cross-field queries; SIR-4 is additionally
separated by field. $\Delta$ and $\Delta_{\mathrm{macro}}$ are the relative
reductions from same- to cross-field performance on TOMATO-Star and
across the four equally weighted SIR-4 fields, respectively. MIR is
reported overall because field labels are unavailable. Green shading
marks the three highest comparable scores per column, while blue shading
marks the three smallest reductions; ties share a shade. $^\star$ marks the
stratified 500-query TOMATO-Star subset and, on SIR-4, same-field cells computed
on 250 sampled queries per field (cross-field cells use every cross-field query;
MOOSE-Star uses the full test sets). $^\ddagger$ screens each query's Qwen3
top-100 pool instead of the corpus (pool recall 64--84\% on SIR-4, 73.7\% on MIR).
$^\dagger$ SIR-4 entries were obtained with the current
training code; the other SIR-4 \textsc{SciGraphIR} rows predate it.}
