\caption{Graph view of Example~2. The query asks why LLM ideas converge; the gold is a 1993 cognitive-psychology

hypothetical answers name the paper, and every dense baseline ranks it beyond 25. In the frame graph the query's

generation process reaches the gold through the \textsc{finding} it reports (route 2). CCMP amplifies the hop into

channel. Dashed arrows are the seeding step; solid arrows are graph edges, $r^{-1}$ the inverse relation; weights
